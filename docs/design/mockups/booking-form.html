What this asserts — argue with it

  • One scrollable page, sticky "Pay & book." The-booking-1's ~6-tap funnel as a single low-friction screen — no wizard, the running total always visible. Xola's booking widget is the pattern; this trims it to what a whole-boat sale needs.
  • Whole boat, one party — and the customer never picks a boat. They pick the offering + a time + guest count; the system assigns an available boat behind the scenes. No deck plan, no hull to choose.
  • Max = the best available boat for that slot (operator, 2026-07-16). "Up to 16 for this time" = the largest boat still free at 1:30, not a fixed number. If only a 12 is left, the max is 12. The guest count they enter is what the claim then fits a boat to.
  • 12 guests included, each extra $40. The included count + extra-guest price are the offering's; the boat only sets the ceiling. Extra guests are sold here — the fix for Xola's "call us for extra tickets." Guest count lives here, not on the availability picker — asking it earlier would gate the customer out of even seeing times.
  • Gratuity is required, three positive tiers, no decline — matching the live Xola config. It's the pre-gratuity of DEC-124, first-class, not an add-on buried in the list; the post-trip "add more" path lives on the booking-link manage page.
  • How you get here: a public offerings page (all experiences) sits in front of this; the customer clicks an offering to launch this form. Leaning toward a virtual modal over that page (Xola-over-our-site style) — good enough for now, but an implementation detail to settle later, not a spec commitment.
  • The phone number earns its place: "for your booking link + crew updates." The booking link (DEC-122 living link) is how they manage + tip post-trip; it's the durable handle, so we say why we want the number.
  • Waiver is the booking guest's consent + linked terms now (DEC-110). Other guests sign their own ahead of the trip via a link the booker shares (from the manage page) — not at the dock. The real e-waiver provider is a later wire-up.
  • Summary is honest about where money goes: fare, add-ons, gratuity (tinted as crew money), tax, service fee. Gratuity is exempt from tax + fee (DEC-124) — the line items reflect that, not a blended number.
  • Deposit at the bottom: the pay bar leads with "Due now $100.00" and a "$100 deposit · $742.28 total" sub-line; the summary breaks out Deposit due now ($100) and a greyed Balance · charged 3 days before ($642.28). Deposit (flat or %) and the balance-charge timing are system admin settings — a flat $100 today, balance is total − deposit. Pay-in-full would be the same bar with no split.
  • Settled 2026-07-17: the deposit (flat or %) and the balance-charge timing are both system admin settings (flat $100, charged 3 days before, today); Flex insurance defaults off (no pre-checked upsell); guests sign the waiver ahead of the trip; the tip block drops its explainer line. "Change" reopens the availability picker.
