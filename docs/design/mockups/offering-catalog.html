Availability is computed from this rule — no rows are written up front.
Open slots = schedule × vessels × dates − blocks − bookings, computed on the fly. A row is materialized only when a slot gets state: booked, given a per-departure override, or held. So a season is a few dozen rows, not thousands — and editing this schedule just recomputes the open slots, nothing to rewrite.
Draft generates no slots. Flip to Live and the slots appear on the reservation calendar.
Blocks / blackout are NOT here (and nothing like Xola's). They live on their own surface as scoped, availability-subtracting blocks: location block (river closed → everything at that location, a date+time window), vessel block (boat out of service → all its slots, a date range), vessel hold (reserve a boat for a private thing, no customer event). A single departure change is a per-departure override on the calendar.

Gratuity is first-class, one table keyed by kind (pre / post) — deliberately NOT an add-on.
Xola's add-on tips have been terrible precisely because an add-on gets taxed and fee'd like revenue. Gratuity isn't revenue — it routes to crew (the xola-tip-extractor union until Xola retires), is exempt from tax + the service fee, and is reported as crew pay, not sales. The kind column is the one honest discriminator; new kinds are a row, not a code change.

Settled this pass (operator, 2026-07-16)

  • Vessels, not a per-boat table. "Runs on" picks boats from the Vessel/equipment list; capacity is a Vessel fact (set once, on the Vessel screen), never overridden here.
  • Virtual slots, not 3,060 rows. The schedule is a rule; availability is computed (schedule × vessels × dates − blocks − bookings). A row exists only once a slot is booked, overridden, or held. Editing the offering recomputes open slots — nothing to rewrite. (This corrects the earlier "generate all events ahead" framing; DEC-123's eager-generation leg gets amended to match.)
  • Mid-season edits: offering edits apply forward to unbooked, unmodified slots (which are virtual → just recompute). A per-departure override wins and is sticky. A booked trip is frozen — price is a contract; a time/capacity change to a booked trip flows through the customer-notify path, never a silent bulk rewrite. Retroactive re-pricing of booked trips is out of P12.
  • “guest,” never “seat.” Seat = a crew position (the crew engine owns it). The reservation side counts guests against Vessel capacity.
  • Gratuity ≠ tips ≠ add-on. Own table, keyed by kind (pre / post). Routes to crew, exempt from tax + fee. Add-ons stay generic for real upsells.
  • Tax + service fee are system settings with a per-offering override (the two-municipalities escape hatch), not primary fields on every offering.
  • Draft / Live / Hidden lifted from bushel: Draft generates no slots; Hidden is a reversible soft-delete (out of browse + default admin list, bookings kept, "Show hidden" toggle) — the only safe way to retire an offering people booked. Same flag will serve customer records.
  • Location is now a field (and its own entity) — surfaced by the "block a location for a date+time" use case. Offering references it; a location block subtracts every slot there.

Still open

  • Two gratuity kinds — pre and post. That's it for now (operator, 2026-07-17). The table's kind column still takes more without a migration if a third ever shows up, but nothing is built for one.
