This is the thing Xola can't do — and it kills two workarounds.
Xola carries one capacity per experience, but your four boats are 12–16. So a party of 14 can't self-serve: the widget shows “CALL TO” on some days and tells the customer “to add extra tickets, contact BrewBoat directly”. Both are the same limitation wearing two hats — a human has to work out which hull fits.
Muster holds capacity per event, and events are per boat. Availability is already party ≤ that boat's max. So a 14-person party is simply shown the boats that fit and books itself. No “CALL TO” state, no phone-in for extra guests — neither is modelled, because neither is a real thing about the business.

What this asserts — argue with it

  • The catalog is global; the calendar is per-event. Departure times, season, days and pricing are set once here and generate every event. Changing one Wednesday's 3:30 happens on the reservation calendar, not here. That's the split you named.
  • Events are generated ahead of the sale. 4 boats × 5 departures × 153 days ≈ 3,060 events for the season. They must exist unsold to be listable as available — which is why 11.3's "create the Event when the webhook lands" has to reverse for Muster-native.
  • Pricing is base + extra guest, not flat. From the live widget: $499 buys the boat for up to 12; extra guests are $40/head to that boat's max. A single flat number can't express it.
  • Capacity is per boat, and that's the point. Xola holds one capacity per experience against your 12–16 hulls, so it falls back on “CALL TO” days and “contact BrewBoat directly” for extra tickets — one limitation wearing two hats. Muster's capacity is per-event and events are per-boat, and availability is already party ≤ Event.capacity, so a party of 14 sees the boats that fit and books itself. Neither workaround is modelled.
  • Price variations are rules, not per-day numbers. Sunday −20% and Prime Saturday +$50 sit on top of Base. Verified against brewcle.com: Sundays render $399.20 = $499 × 0.8.
  • Tax and service fee live here, per Offering, showing their rate — so they read as settings, which is what the reservation calendar's 5.2% / 3% tags point back to.
  • "Runs on" is the boat set, not a per-boat schedule. Four boats × one schedule is what BrewBoat actually does, and it's what makes the calendar a clean 4-column grid.
  • Questionnaire is cut (your call). So is store credit, and the Reports/Marketing/Distribution/App Store nav.
  • “CALL TO” is resolved — it's a Xola limitation, not a state (operator, 2026-07-16). Not modelled. Same for phone-in extra tickets.

Where does the Muster-owned-vessel-day marking (DEC-106) get set — here, implicitly from "Runs on" + Season, or on its own screen? Do price variations ever stack (Sunday and a holiday)?
