What this asserts — argue with it

  • Third admin surface, beside calendar + shift board (DEC-123 §3). Calendar answers "what's sold, when"; this answers "who paid, how much, refund/resend." Two tabs: Purchases (orders) and Customers (contacts).
  • An order is the money view of a reservation. The detail pane here (payment, actions) is the same content the reservation-calendar's detail pane shows — keyed by order instead of by departure. One reservation, two ways in.
  • Gratuity is a separate line that routes to crew (DEC-124), never blended into revenue totals — the operator sees crew money as crew money.
  • Refund hands off to Stripe. Cards-on-file are Stripe's; we don't rebuild card storage. Operator-initiated refund here; customer self-refund (Flex) happens from their booking link (DEC-107, amended).
  • "Party" is the whole boat, not a per-guest charge. You buy the boat, not a count of guests. Booking guest + "up to 16"; individual guest names collected at the dock; "Print roster" produces the dock check-in sheet. (Reservation-side people are guests or passengers — never "seat" (crew-only), never "head".)
  • Customer is a contact record, not an account — no login, reused Draft/Hidden soft-delete. Lifetime + history give the operator a repeat-guest view Xola buried.
  • One order = one boat = one reservation (operator, 2026-07-17). Want two boats? That's two orders. No multi-reservation order object to build — the "order" and the "reservation" are the same row for now. (The schema still allows an Event to carry more later, but nothing here assumes it.)
  • A customer is keyed by phone (operator, 2026-07-17) — but keep phone as a unique lookup key, not the literal primary key. The customer row gets a stable surrogate id; normalized phone (E.164) carries a UNIQUE constraint for dedup + the recovery-flow lookup. Why not phone-as-PK: a number changes or gets recycled by the carrier, and if it's the PK you'd have to migrate the row and every reservation FK — and a recycled number would inherit the old customer's history. Surrogate-PK-plus-unique-phone gives you phone-as-identity (same phone = same customer, so "merge duplicate contacts" mostly dissolves) without welding a mutable value into the key. Same instinct as the Reservation.eventId guardrail: don't make a business fact load-bearing structure.
  • "Message" routes through the customer sender, never the crew line (operator, 2026-07-17). A customer never texts the crew number — separate sender number + 10DLC campaign, so a customer spam-report can't take down the crew channel. Tracked in #119 (second sender number — crew vs customer isolation). Muster sends informational messages only; the second number protects the crew channel, it doesn't enable marketing.
