Changed from v1 — your notes

  • Density is real now. 4 boats × 5 slots = 20/day. Day is the default view and the grid is boat-columns × time-rows. A week is 140 events — that's what the filter is for, and why Week can't be the default.
  • Open / Booked / Blacked-out filter, with counts, next to the view toggle.
  • Blackout shows as a first-class state on the grid (3:30 Brew 3). Blacking out one event happens here. Blacking out future events in bulk is a separate admin screen — not this one.
  • Sell from here. Open slots carry a Sell button — admin sells from this screen, so it's one click from the grid.
  • Crewed moved to the top, next to the boat, as a link to the shift. Shows 2/2 so a short-crewed trip is visible without leaving.
  • "Roster" is gone. The guest list is Guests. The person who made the reservation is Booking guest.
  • Gratuity is its own tax-exempt line, not an add-on (DEC-124, fixed 2026-07-17) — crew money, never taxed or fee'd, matching the catalog + booking form. Add-ons (Flex, etc.) stay their own section with + Add add-on.
  • Tax and service fee show their configured rate (5.2%, 3%) so it's obvious they're settings, not hardcoded.
  • Catalog vs. this screen: the catalog creates event times that apply globally; this screen changes an individual event. Same split Xola has, minus the seat counting.
  • Settled 2026-07-17: "Booking guest" is the term (the person who reserved); tax + service fee are a system setting with a per-Offering override (matching the catalog); gratuity is tax-exempt crew money, not an add-on.
  • Settled 2026-07-17: Sell opens an inline modal booking form on the calendar (fast phone-booking; operator flagged modals can be fiddly — revisit if it annoys in practice). Week view approved (days × time-slots, 4-boat dots).
