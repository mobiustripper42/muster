is modelled, subtracted by the deriver, counted by `computeBlockImpact`, rendered read-only by the
registry with a forward link, and named in `block-admin.ts:8` as "made on the calendar (#464)".
`saveBlockAdmin` has refused the kind with `bad_kind` since 12.10. **Nothing could create one.**
The registry's empty state has told operators to "make a hold on the calendar" for two phases —
advice nobody could follow.

**The operator's own read of it:** *"I'm not sure if this is a real feature I need, it's a side
effect of xola reservation system."* Correct, and worth recording — Xola's blackout model is
per-slot, DEC-125 kept a single-slot kind to absorb it. If it goes unused post-cutover the kind
is one `case` in three files to retire.

**Test-first, and the first one bit.** The 11 domain cases failed on `is not a function`; the e2e
failed on `hold-confirm` not found. The round-trip e2e asserts release as well as hold on purpose
— asserting only the hold passes with a one-way door, which is the version nobody can undo.

**The multi-offering test ran straight into #702.** With two offerings on one boat-time the cards
are drawn exactly on top of each other, so Playwright couldn't click the lower one — and neither
can a human. The real assertion turned out to be better anyway: both cards carry the SAME href
(`new Set(hrefs).size === 1`), which is what "keyed on the physical slot, not the offering" means
and what would fail if it weren't. Left #702 alone.

**Two things the screenshots caught that the tests didn't.** JSX ate the space in "2 offerings"
(the e2e then caught it, having been written to assert the visible string). And the Held card was
byte-identical to a Blackout card — both dark, only one undoable from here. Gave the hold an
accent border so the legend isn't the only thing saying which dark cards click.

**No DEC.** DEC-125 already decided the vessel hold is a single-slot block with no `Event`, and
`block-admin.ts` already named the calendar as its home. Nothing changes; this wires a decided
thing. Same line as last session: a defect or an unbuilt decision gets built, a changed holding
gets an amendment.

**Code review:** Clean bill of health, pressed on six named risks and cleared each with citations
