## Task 2: Make a chargeback visible instead of counting it as revenue (issues #723, #777)

**Completed:**
- **The real defect was `countsAsPaid`, not the missing webhook event.** It was a deny-list
  (`status !== "refunded"`) under a comment claiming the compiler would force a revisit when the
  union widened "to disputes or chargebacks" — it would not have, and adding `disputed` would
  have counted a chargeback as money in five places at once. Now an allow-list
  (`src/reservations/payment-config.ts`).
- `disputed` + `dispute_lost` on `PaymentStatus`; `dispute_updated` port event mapping Stripe's
  eight dispute statuses to four via an exhaustive switch with no `default`; `markPaymentDisputed`
  on the port and both adapters; `recordDispute` in the webhook.
- Money alerts now TEXT every active admin (`src/adapters/forward-money-alert.ts`,
  `alertMoneyProblem` in `app/lib/alert.ts`). `alertPaidButUnbooked` was a `console.error`
  carrying a TODO, so every money alert reached nobody not reading Vercel logs.
- issue #777 folded in: alert copy is plain ASCII at the source. **An earlier cut sanitized at
  the send boundary and the operator rejected it** — we are the only author of these strings, so
  the fix is not to write them, not to launder them at the door. A GSM-7 checker and a test-double
  guard were both built and then deleted on the same reasoning.
- `disputed` PaymentState + filter chip on `/admin/purchases`; `DetailMoney.disputed` and a
  "Chargeback" row on the calendar detail pane.
- `docs/DEPLOY.md` lists the three `charge.dispute.*` subscriptions — the section exists because a
  missing subscription is the one production failure that is silent.

**Code review:** 5 findings, 4 fixed. The serious one was mine: excluding a disputed payment
re-opens the balance the way a refund does, and the balance-link button only guarded refunds — so
the pane offered to re-bill a customer mid-chargeback and `createBalanceCheckout` would have
minted it. Now refused server-side (`reason: "disputed"`) as well as hidden. Also fixed:
`dispute_lost` is terminal in both adapters (Stripe does not guarantee delivery order).
**Known limitation, in the PR body:** a stale `updated` after a WIN can still flip the row back
to `disputed` — a win writes `succeeded`, indistinguishable from never-disputed. Fixing it needs
the dispute id + timestamp on the row, i.e. a migration.
