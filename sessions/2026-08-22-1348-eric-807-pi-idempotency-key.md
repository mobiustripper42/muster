## Task 1: SPEC §2.8 — Booking & Payment, written fresh

**Completed:**
- `docs/SPEC.md` — §2.8 Booking & Payment added (~200 lines), the reservations section DEC-105 booked
  and issue #565 tracked. Reserved at line 1474 with a placeholder since the audit; never written.
  Written as the design we would choose knowing nothing about the current code.
- The model: **one record**. A `Reservation` is written `pending` before payment and it is what
  occupies the departure; expiry, then `confirmed` or `expired`. No second object claims a slot.
- §2.8.2 — the pending row names a **slot**, not an Event; `eventId` empty until confirm. Dissolves the
  phantom-events problem two independent second-opinion passes each called "the real design question."
- §2.8.2a — occupancy is the **hull over the trip's duration**, never the exact triple; hull-day
  serialization; **one** occupancy rule for reader and writer. Added after review — see below.
- §2.8.4 — metadata is decorative and **nothing reads it, not even a pointer**; the link is the
  PaymentIntent id on our own row. Scoped to the booking charge; DEC-134's `purpose` guard stays.
- §2.8.6 — the sweeper marks rows `expired`, never deletes, so the payment window becomes measurable.
- `docs/SPEC.md` §4 — owed-unlocks banner rewritten: one unlock paid, one withdrawn on the finding that
  the audit's stated reason did not hold.
- **No new decision record, deliberately.** §2.8 is the design; DEC-105 booked it and the spec text
  discharges it. Reconciling DEC-107/109/125/134 against §2.8 is the next piece of work.

**Code review:** 5 findings + 2 cleanups. Four fixed in `50998b3`, one answered. The load-bearing one:
§2.8 identified a pending reservation by `(vessel, date, time)` and said nothing about **overlap** —
the guard DEC-109's issue #691 correction added after a real production double-booking (`won, won` in
3 of 3 runs against real Postgres). An implementer following only §2.8 would have reopened it. Also
caught a self-contradiction in the failure table — a declined card *is*
`payment_intent.payment_failed`, so "expire immediately" destroyed the retry the reservation exists to
support — and one unfalsifiable claim ("expired rows are only generated by real customers", disproved
by issue #799 / issue #806). The misread: DEC-109's "keep them separate" is hold-vs-**Block**, not
hold-vs-reservation.
