**Diagnosed from a CI failure on someone else's PR.** Lane B's PR #862 went red on e2e; it changes
**five markdown/YAML files and no code**, so it could not have caused anything. The demo window sat
on the 10th–16th of next month — a calendar slot, not a lead time — so the distance from today to
the first booking swings from ~42 days on the 1st to 14 on the 29th to 12 on the 31st. Inside 14
days a customer-asked cancel is non-refundable, so the two quotes `calendar.spec.ts` asserts
*differ* stopped differing: $0.00 against an unchanged $588.80, which is exactly the asymmetry the
log showed.

**The sharpest part is the time-of-day dependence.** The terms compare instants and the trip departs
at 13:30, so a 14-calendar-day gap is under 14 days for any run starting after 13:30. Green at
09:00, red at 17:00 — which is why `main` was green nineteen hours earlier with no code in between.

**The suite was already carrying the bug.** The new invariant failed on `2027-06-30`, a date that
was in the test's `TODAYS` list before this branch. Under the old anchor it put the first booking 12
days out — inside the real cutoff, not merely under the new floor. Nothing had ever asserted it.

**Fixed by removing a literal, not updating one.** Code review found the change had left the old
"10th–16th" in eight comments, including `seed-reservation.ts`'s module docstring contradicting its
own function comment 100 lines below. Each now describes the shape and points at the code for the
days, so the next move of the window cannot strand another eight copies.
