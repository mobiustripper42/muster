### Standing rules this session learned the hard way — read these first

Written 2026-09-01 before a deliberate compaction, so a summarized reader picks up where this one left
off instead of rediscovering. Each of these cost at least one review finding.

1. **An absence or count claim is written as the command plus its output, never as prose.** *"Nothing
